Since AI 2026 – Event Photography & Video

Since AI is the largest hackathon in the Nordics. More than a thousand participants from around the world spend two intensive days building and designing solutions to real AI challenges.

Abstrakti handled the full content production: planning, photo and video coverage, and post-production. We delivered the YouTube aftermovie, a social media aftermovie, short-form videos, interviews, and an image bank Since AI will use to market its 2027 event.

A team of three photographers and videographers covered the event.
