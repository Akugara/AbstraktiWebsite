Nose Job Saaristo – Video
A Nose Job for Soile is a project in collaboration with Björkhölm Cottages for the Saaristo campaign.

We shot a creative video telling the story of Soile, a sheep in the archipelago who would love to travel around and discover the smells of other islands. With a hint of wholesome humor, we launched a 2-day production with lovely storytelling.
