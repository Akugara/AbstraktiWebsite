lush 2026 – Event Photography

Slush brings together Europe's most ambitious founders, investors and innovators. It's one of the world's leading startup conferences.

As part of the official photography team, we covered the scale and energy of the event. That meant keynotes and panels, startup pitches, networking sessions and the atmosphere around the venue. Speakers ranged from Alexander Stubb, President of Finland, to Lovable's CEO.

Working closely with the other photographers, we captured the moments that showed what Slush is about: a diverse community and new ideas in action.
